Baby unit

Put this device near the cot with the camera facing the baby, and keep it plugged in.

Sound and movement are analysed on this device only. Nothing is recorded or stored, unless you switch on "Movement clips" (off by default), which keeps short clips in this page's memory only. Audio/video is sent only to your parent device, directly over your home Wi-Fi, and only while you tap Listen or Watch there. When a parent taps Talk, their voice plays from this device's speaker.
